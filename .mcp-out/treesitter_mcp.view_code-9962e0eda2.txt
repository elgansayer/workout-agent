   1 | """Central, fail-closed redaction for logs, diagnostics, and error responses.
   2 | 
   3 | Every process that can handle credentials or user data installs this layer
   4 | (``install_log_redaction``) so the same rules apply to application loggers,
   5 | third-party loggers (uvicorn, httpx, SDKs), exception tracebacks, and uncaught
   6 | exceptions. The field vocabulary comes from :mod:`data_classification`; this
   7 | module adds the value-shape rules (bearer tokens, API-key formats, OAuth codes,
   8 | cookies, e-mail addresses) that apply even when a secret appears in free text
   9 | with no field name next to it.
  10 | 
  11 | Redaction is deliberately conservative: a false positive costs a little
  12 | debugging context, a false negative leaks a credential or personal data.
  13 | """
